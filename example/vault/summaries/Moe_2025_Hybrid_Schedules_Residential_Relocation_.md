## Research question

Do employees on hybrid schedules move farther from their workplace than
employees who commute daily? (p. 1)

---

## Theoretical framework

Residential choice under a relaxed commuting constraint (p. 4).

---

## Methods

### Design

Cross-sectional comparison of recent movers by schedule type (p. 6).

### Data

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Source** | Synthetic register of relocations linked to an employer survey (p. 6) |
| **N** | 1,150 recent movers (p. 7) |

### Analytic strategy

Linear models of distance moved away from the workplace, by schedule type
(p. 9).

---

## Key findings

- Hybrid employees moved on average 6.2 km farther from the workplace (p. 12)
- The difference shrinks to 3.0 km once housing prices are controlled (p. 14)

---

## Implications

- Part of the apparent relocation effect is a housing-price effect.

---

## Limitations

- Cross-sectional; schedule type may follow the move (p. 25).
- Working-paper version; numbers may change in print (reader's note).

---

## Abstract

Hybrid employees live farther from work, but much of the gap reflects where
housing is affordable.

---

## Quotable
